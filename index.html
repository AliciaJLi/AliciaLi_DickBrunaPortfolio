picture book gallery
hello, I am dick bruna

a dutch writer, illustrator, and designer

I create simple stories with drawings

in the eyes of a four-year-old
what do my works have in common?

they are simple, like pictograms

bunny or elephant, both are easy

only the important lines remain
when my grandchildren visit me

or when I cycle past children in the street

I want to create a world for children

for their imagination
stories about playing in a tent

or stories about death and grief

I am honest with children

as they are honest to me
